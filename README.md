# DSA-DATAANALYSIS-PROJECT

## PROJECT TITLE - AMAZON ANALYSIS
This is an analysis of the highest-rated items reveal a strong consumer preference for affordable yet functional gadgets. Most of the products that earned a rating of 4.7 and above belong to everyday electronics and kitchen appliances. Most users showed high levels of satisfaction even with budget-friendly items, such as USB cables and milk frothers, which received top marks.

Among the standout performers is the Swiffer instant water heater, which  has a near-perfect score but also  an impressive number of reviews indicating both popularity and consistent user satisfaction. Similarly, the Sony Bravia 65" 4K TV, although premium-priced, maintains a high customer rating, suggesting that quality electronics continue to attract and satisfy a loyal audience.

The majority of these well-rated items are heavily discounted—often by more than 50%—suggesting that buyers feel they’re getting great value for money, which likely contributes to positive feedback. Overall, customers tend to reward practicality, reliability, and good pricing with high ratings.


### DATA SOURCES
The dataset used for this analysis was sourced from Amazon India (Amazon.in). It consists of product listings across various categories, including electronics, kitchen appliances, and accessories. The data includes product names, pricing details, discounts, customer ratings, and reviews, making it suitable for analyzing buyer behavior and product performance trends on a major e-commerce platform.

# DSA-DATAANALYSIS-PROJECT

## PROJECT TITLE - KMS ANALYSIS
Data-Driven Insights from Kultra Mega Stores (2009–2012)
This project analyzed over 8,000 order records from KMS. It aimed to uncover trends in customer behavior, shipping cost efficiency, and product performance using SQL.
Office Supplies led all product categories in total sales. This shows they are essential purchases, especially for business clients.Ontario, British Columbia, and Alberta had the strongest sales, while Yukon and Nunavut contributed the least. These regions may require tailored strategies.Appliances in Ontario sold strongly. This indicates a customer preference for long-term utility products in that province.These customers placed the smallest total orders. Targeting them with bundles or discounts could help improve overall sales.Regular Air and Delivery Truck were the most expensive shipping methods. Some of these costs were not justified by order urgency.
Top 10 customers focused on Binders, Appliances, and Paper products. They represent high lifetime value and should be retained actively.Top small business and corporate customers were identified. They help drive stable revenue and deserve specialized service strategies.The most profitable customer was in the consumer segment. Targeting similar profiles can increase overall profit margins.
Some low-priority orders used expensive shipping. This mismatch reveals logistics inefficiencies that can be improved.
In conclusion, KMS should align shipping decisions with order priority, focus on top regions, and re-engage low-volume customers to maximize sales.

###  DATA SOURCE

The dataset used in this project is from Kultra Mega Stores (KMS), covering the years 2009 to 2012 which contains their customer order record. 
The data includes:
- Order details: dates, quantities, prices, discounts, profit
- Customer and regional info
- Shipping methods and costs
- Product categories and segments
An additional table containing return statuses (`Order_Status`) was also referenced for extended analysis.
